About This Course

This is a project-first, practical course designed to take you from MediaPipe basics to building real-world computer vision applications. You'll learn by doing, with each module featuring hands-on projects you can add to your portfolio.

Whether you're a beginner looking to get started with computer vision or an intermediate developer wanting to master MediaPipe, this course provides step-by-step guidance with video tutorials, source code, and deployment tips.
